How to play

Type any valid five-letter word and press Enter. Every tile changes colour to tell you how close you were.

Right letter, right spot

BRAVE

Green: B is in the word and in the correct spot.

Right letter, wrong spot

TIGER

Yellow: I is in the word, but somewhere else.

Not in the word

MOUSE

Grey: U is not in the word at all.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

What data do you collect?

None. There are no accounts and no analytics. Your settings and statistics are stored only on your device. Read the full privacy policy.

Where does the daily word come from?

The app asks a third-party word service for today's word, once per day. Like any web request, that service can see your IP address. If it can't be reached, the app uses a built-in word for that date instead, so you can always play. The privacy policy explains this in detail.

Will I get the same word as my friends?

Usually yes, when the daily word can be fetched. When you are offline, the built-in word for the day is used, and it may differ from the one your friends get online.

Can I replay today's puzzle?

No. Once you finish the daily puzzle, it is locked until tomorrow, but practice mode is always available.

Is this affiliated with The New York Times?

No. Daily Word Guess is an independent game and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by The New York Times Company or any other word-game publisher.
