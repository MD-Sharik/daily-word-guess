Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app “Daily Word Guess” (package com.dailywordguess.game) and this website.

In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your settings and statistics stay on your device. The only thing the app sends over the internet is one request per day to a third-party service to get the daily word, and that service can see your IP address.

1. Who we are

Daily Word Guess is published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.

2. What we collect

We do not collect personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and the app asks for no personal details. We do not run a server that receives data from the app, and we do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software of our own.

3. What is stored on your device

The app saves the following only in its private storage on your phone:

  • your settings (dark theme, high-contrast mode, hard mode, whether you have seen the tutorial);
  • your game statistics (games played, wins, streaks, guess distribution);
  • today's puzzle progress, so a finished daily puzzle cannot be replayed and an unfinished one can be resumed;
  • the daily word received from the word service, cached for that calendar day.

This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. The app does not allow cloud backup of this data.

4. The daily-word request (third party)

To get the word of the day, the app makes a secure (HTTPS) request to a third-party word service at wordlehintstoday.org. The request is made at most once per day, when you open the app and no word for today has been cached yet. The app sends no account, advertising or device identifiers and no game data in this request.

As with any internet request, the operator of that service can see your IP address and the standard technical details of the request (such as the time and the app's user-agent string, DailyWordGuess/1.0). We do not control that service, do not receive that information, and cannot speak for how the operator handles it; please refer to the operator's own privacy information. If the service cannot be reached or returns something the app cannot use, the app falls back to a word built into the app and makes no further request until the next time you open it.

5. Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google processes information about your installation and device under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Google may provide us with aggregated statistics (for example install counts) and, if you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data on your device, anonymised crash or performance reports in the Play Console. We do not receive your name, email address or any other direct identifier through this.

6. Permissions

PermissionWhy
INTERNETTo request the daily word (section 4). Nothing else uses the network.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. The “Share” button copies your result as text to the clipboard; the app never reads the clipboard.

7. Children

Daily Word Guess is a general-audience word game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app has no accounts or advertising, there is no personal information to collect or remove.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any. The only third party that receives anything from the app is the word service described in section 4.

9. Retention and your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your on-device data is yours to delete at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California) you may have rights over personal data held by others, such as the word-service operator; you should contact that operator for those requests. You are welcome to email us with any privacy question.

10. Security

The daily-word request uses HTTPS. Data saved on your device lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally read.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app works in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above, and the new version will apply from the date shown.

12. Not affiliated

Daily Word Guess is an independent game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by The New York Times Company.
